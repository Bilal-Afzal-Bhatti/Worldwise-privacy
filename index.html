Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 01, 2026

Welcome to WorldWise (“Know the World”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information you may share while using our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

WorldWise is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not require you to create an account, register, or log in to use the app. As a result:

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.
  • The app operates locally on your device to cache data, track your quiz scores, and record seen countries/territories.

2. Permissions & Device Data

Our app may request standard device permissions strictly necessary for optimal performance and rendering (such as network access to load map layouts or geographical databases). We do not track, store, or sell your device identifiers or usage logs.

3. Third-Party Services

WorldWise may utilize standard app store analytics or distribution platforms (such as Google Play Services) which may automatically collect non-personal diagnostic data, crash reports, and installation statistics as governed by their respective privacy policies.

4. Children’s Privacy

WorldWise is safe for users of all ages. Because we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, parents and guardians can feel secure letting children explore the globe through our app.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. You are advised to review this policy periodically for any updates.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out via our developer contact channels.
